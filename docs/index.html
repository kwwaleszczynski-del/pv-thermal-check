1. What the site sensors say

Ambient sensor stuck at 0 degF

Share of readings exactly at 0 degF by month; the analysis uses the airport station (NOAA ISD, 14 km) for air temperature and 10 m wind instead.

POA sensor reads high

Measured / clear-sky POA on clear days: the sensor reads about 18 % above the model, so irradiance is rescaled before any fitting.

2. Module temperature models

Test error (fitted on 2017, scored on 2016)

Against the back-of-module sensor. Published defaults overpredict this sensor by several kelvin.

Error by time of day

Mornings are too warm in every steady-state model: after a clear desert night the module starts below air temperature. A first-order lag helps a little.

3. Is the module sensor right? Voc thermometry

Inverter down, array at open circuit

During the June 2016 outage the DC voltage channel reads the string open-circuit voltage, which gives the cell temperature through the module's voltage coefficient (24 days with open-circuit data in total).

Rise over air vs irradiance

The sensor reports a fraction of the heating the cells really see. Fitted to the cells: PVsyst Uc = 21.3, Uv = 1.35 W/m2K.

4. Temperature losses and performance ratio

Monthly temperature loss

Irradiance-weighted loss versus 25 degC with the module's -0.458 %/K power coefficient.

Monthly DC performance ratio

Raw PR falls every summer. Correcting to 25 degC (IEC 61724-1) or to the plant's mean cell temperature (NREL) takes most of the seasonal swing out. Sun above 30 degrees only, to keep winter row shading out.

5. Sensor fault detection

The real sensor

Daily ratio of measured to modelled temperature rise. Against a model fitted to its own history the sensor looks healthy; against the Voc-calibrated physics it has been reading low all along.

Injected faults: time to alarm

Faults injected into the real 2016 record at 40 start dates; CUSUM on the daily residual and rise ratio, threshold set for at most one false alarm per year.

6. Inverter

Inverter temperature residual

Model: temperature = constant + load term + air term; the air term is zero (fan-regulated cooling). After the repair that ended the June 2016 outage the unit runs -0.7 K cooler at the same load.

Thermal derating search

A step is flagged when AC power sits on a plateau more than 8 % below the expected output. The real record shows no capped days; an injected cap is found.

7. Step-up transformer (IEC 60076-7 model)

Hot-spot in Warsaw's hottest week

Generic ONAN distribution transformer fed by a PV plant with DC/AC 1.2 (ERA5 weather), for three transformer ratings.

Insulation ageing vs transformer size

Days of life consumed per year (365 = nominal rate). PV loads a transformer only around midday, so it can be smaller than the inverter.
